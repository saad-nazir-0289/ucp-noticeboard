# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **UCP NoticeBoard** ("the Extension") collects, uses, and protects user information.

## 1. Overview

UCP NoticeBoard is a Chrome extension that displays approved university notices directly within the University of Central Punjab (UCP) Student Portal.

The Extension is intended solely for use with the UCP Student Portal.

## 2. Information We Collect

The Extension collects only the information necessary to provide its functionality:

* **Student Roll Number** – Read from the UCP Student Portal after the user has logged in. The Roll Number is used to identify the user, determine their role (Student, Publisher, or Administrator), and retrieve the appropriate notices and permissions from the backend.

If a user creates a notice, the information they choose to submit (such as notice title, description, and optional image URL) is stored as part of the notice.

No other personal information is intentionally collected.

## 3. How We Use Information

Collected information is used only to:

* Identify the logged-in user.
* Determine user permissions.
* Display notices relevant to the user.
* Allow authorized users to create, edit, or delete notices.
* Provide administrative features such as role management and basic usage analytics.

The information is **not** used for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

## 4. Information We Do Not Collect

The Extension does **not** collect or access:

* Passwords or login credentials.
* Financial or payment information.
* Health information.
* Emails, messages, or personal communications.
* GPS or precise location.
* Browsing history outside the UCP Student Portal.
* Keystrokes or keyboard input.
* Mouse movements or click tracking unrelated to the Extension's functionality.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade user information.

Information is shared only with the Extension's backend service hosted at **[BACKEND_DOMAIN](https://ucp-noticeboard-api-production.up.railway.app)** for the purpose of providing the Extension's functionality.

We do not share user data with advertisers or third parties for marketing purposes.

## 6. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information transmitted between the Extension and the backend, including encrypted HTTPS connections where supported.

## 7. Data Retention

User information is retained only as long as necessary to operate the Extension.

Notices remain stored until removed by an authorized user or administrator. Expired notices are hidden from normal viewing but may remain stored unless deleted.

## 8. Third-Party Services

The Extension communicates only with its own backend service to retrieve and manage notice data.

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be published at this page.
