# Posting from WhatsApp, Instagram and email

Publishers and Admins can turn a message, post or email into a notice
without retyping it. NoticeBoard reads what you give it **on your phone**
(nothing is sent anywhere until you tap Publish), fills in the title,
details, link, deadline and category it can find, and you check the form
before publishing.

## Android: Share to NoticeBoard

One-time setup: open the NoticeBoard site in Chrome and tap **Install app**
(or ⋮ → *Add to Home screen*). Installing is what makes NoticeBoard appear
in Android's share menu.

| From | What to do | What gets filled in |
|---|---|---|
| **WhatsApp** message | Long-press the message → **Share** → **NoticeBoard** | Title (first line), details, link, deadline, category |
| **WhatsApp** poster image | Open the image → **Share** → **NoticeBoard** | The image becomes the poster; its text is read to fill the form |
| **Instagram** post | Take a **screenshot** of the post and share the screenshot to NoticeBoard. Sharing the post itself works too, but Instagram only sends a link | From a screenshot: title from the caption, details, deadline. From a shared post: just the link |
| **Outlook** / Gmail email | Select the email's text → **Share** → **NoticeBoard** (or copy it and use *Paste text*, below). Attachments such as a poster can be shared the same way | Title from the subject or first line, details, link, deadline |

## iPhone and computers: Quick fill

iPhone doesn't let web apps appear in the share menu, and desktop browsers
mostly don't either, so the **New notice** form has a **Quick fill** box
that works everywhere:

- **Paste text** — copy a WhatsApp message or email first, then tap it.
  (If the browser asks, allow pasting; if it can't read the clipboard, a
  box appears to paste into.)
- **Read screenshot** — pick a screenshot or photo of a poster; its text is
  read and used to fill the form, and the image becomes the poster.

## Good to know

- Always check the form: dates are read in Pakistan's day/month order
  (20/10 = 20 October), and a date next to words like *deadline*, *last
  date*, *register by* or *closes* is preferred over other dates in the
  message. Dates without a year are taken as the next time that date comes
  round. Without a time, the deadline is set to 11:59 PM.
- The category is only picked when the message clearly fits one; otherwise
  it's left for you to choose.
- Reading screenshots downloads a ~7 MB text-recognition engine the first
  time, from NoticeBoard's own site, then works from the phone's cache.
- Students who share something to NoticeBoard just see a note that only
  Publishers can post.
